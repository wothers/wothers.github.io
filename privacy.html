Chromapanels Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Chromapanels puzzle game ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. The app is made by wothers ("we", "us").

The short version

  • There are no accounts, and we never ask for your name, email address or any other personal details.
  • Your progress, stats, shards and settings are saved only on your device. We don't receive them.
  • We don't use analytics, tracking or crash reporting of our own.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some information from your device to show and measure ads, as described below. Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent first.
  • Daily puzzle reminders are optional, created on your device, and send nothing to us.

Information stored on your device

To let you pick up where you left off, the app saves the following in your device's storage:

  • Game progress: the levels you've completed, your daily puzzle history and streaks, shards, and auto moves you've bought.
  • Statistics, such as moves made and levels solved.
  • Settings, such as theme, volume, vibration, target hints and whether daily reminders are on.

This information never leaves your device and isn't sent to us or anyone else. It isn't linked to your identity. You can erase it at any time with Settings → Reset progress (which keeps only your settings), or by uninstalling the app. Depending on your device's backup settings, your phone may include the app's data in your own device backups (for example iCloud or Google backups). These backups are handled by Apple or Google under your account, not by us.

The daily puzzle is created on your device from the date. Everyone gets the same puzzle without the app contacting a server.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and is supported by ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen, and an occasional full-screen ad between levels. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, part of Google LLC (or Google Ireland Limited if you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland).

What Google may collect

To serve, measure and personalise ads, and to prevent fraud, Google's Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect:

  • Device and advertising identifiers. On Android, this includes your device's advertising ID. On iPhone and iPad, the app doesn't ask for permission to track you, so Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) isn't available to it.
  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your approximate location (for example, your country or city).
  • Interactions with ads, such as views and taps.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, and general device information such as model, operating system and language.

We don't receive this information in a form that identifies you. AdMob only gives us combined reports, such as how many ads were shown. To learn how Google uses information from apps that show its ads, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your consent and choices

  • If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form before any ads load, so you can choose whether your information is used for personalised ads. No ads load until you've made a choice. You can change it later in Settings → Ads → Privacy choices.
  • Wherever you are, you can limit ad personalisation or reset your advertising ID in your device settings:
    • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).
    • iPhone and iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising, and Tracking.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (rated PG or below).

The web version of the game, if you play it in a browser, doesn't show ads.

Daily puzzle reminders

If you turn on Settings → Notifications → Daily puzzle reminders, the app asks your device for permission to show notifications. It then schedules a reminder on your device each morning. These are local notifications: they're created by the app on your phone, not sent from a server. No device token or other information is sent to us or to a notification service. You can turn reminders off in the app's settings or in your device's notification settings at any time.

Device permissions

Permission Why
Notifications Only if you turn on daily puzzle reminders.
Internet access To load ads. The game itself works without a connection.
Advertising ID (Android) Used by Google AdMob as described above.
Vibration Gentle haptic feedback while playing. You can turn it off in Settings.
Run at startup (Android) Restores scheduled daily reminders after your phone restarts.
Change audio settings (Android) Used by the sound library to play the game's sound effects.

The app doesn't use your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location.

Sharing and selling

We don't sell your personal information, and we don't share information with anyone other than Google, for the advertising described above. We don't receive any information we could share.

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you reset it or uninstall the app. Information collected by Google for advertising is sent over encrypted connections and kept according to Google's own policies.

Children

Chromapanels isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, in the EEA, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your personal information. We don't hold personal information about you, so the game data on your device is fully under your control, as described above. For information collected by Google for advertising, you can use the choices above or contact Google through its privacy policy. If you have a question or request, contact us using the details below.

Changes to this policy

If the app starts handling information differently, we'll update this policy and change the effective date at the top. For significant changes, we'll also say so in the app or on its store page.
